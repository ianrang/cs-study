# Expected Observations

| Question | Expected |
|---|---|
| Q1 | ERP DB: reduce with privileged access review/MFA/logging. Web: reduce with content validation and non-executable upload path. HR: reduce with least privilege/access review. DNS: reduce by restricting zone transfer. |
| Q2 | Resident registration number consent-only basis, MD5, 3-month log retention, plaintext FTP, permanent retention are suspicious or weak. |
| Q3 | A good answer maps collection/storage/provision/destruction to legal basis, encryption, access log retention, secure transfer, retention/destruction policy. |
| Q4 | Proportionality, lawfulness, purpose limitation, transparency, safety, data subject control. |
| Q5 | Legal basis, retention periods, current law/guideline text, biometric principles, CISO/IDC obligations. |
